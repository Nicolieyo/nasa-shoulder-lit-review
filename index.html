About this collection

Built for focused, transparent research

This library supports research on predictive modeling of standardized shoulder movements using surface electromyography and inertial measurement units.

The collection emphasizes scaption, abduction, internal and external rotation, sensor placement, signal processing, muscle fatigue, and automated ergonomic assessment.

This public site contains published literature only. It does not contain participant information, consent materials, or raw research data.
